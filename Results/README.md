Results
Overview

This folder contains the processed outputs generated from differential gene expression and functional enrichment analyses.

Files
DEGs.csv

Complete differential expression results generated using DESeq2.

Significant_DEGs.csv

Genes satisfying significance thresholds after multiple testing correction.

GO_Results.csv

Gene Ontology enrichment results highlighting significantly enriched biological processes.

KEGG_Results.csv

Pathway enrichment results identifying significantly affected biological pathways.

Top_Upregulated_Genes.csv

Most significantly upregulated genes in Day 8 cardiomyocytes compared with Day 4.

Top_Downregulated_Genes.csv

Most significantly downregulated genes in Day 8 cardiomyocytes compared with Day 4.

Biological Interpretation

The analysis identified genes and pathways associated with:

Cardiomyocyte differentiation
Cell fate specification
Tissue morphogenesis
Hormone regulation
Cholesterol metabolism
PPAR signaling
Integrin signaling
Steroid biosynthesis

These findings provide insight into molecular mechanisms involved in cardiac development and maturation.
